## `> ./about`

I am a **Ph.D. student in Software Engineering at the University of Texas at Dallas**, interested in **AI for Software Engineering (AI4SE)**, software design and analysis, and human-computer interaction.

My research goal is to make AI-driven software development more **reliable, secure, and maintainable** by enabling AI systems to reason about software design, dependencies, and quality—not only functional correctness.

Previously, I was a **Lecturer at East West University** and a **Software Engineer at Samsung R&D**. I earned my **B.Sc. and M.Sc. in Software Engineering from the University of Dhaka**, ranking **first in my class in both programs**.
